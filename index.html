About

That process discipline comes from outside email, too — handling compliance cases at HSBC, and billing and invoice operations on SAP at TCS and Genpact. I bring the same structured, checklist-driven approach to a Tuesday send calendar as I did to a fraud alert queue.

Process

How a send actually ships.

The same four steps, every week — which is why the calendar never slips.

Step 01

Segment

Pull the audience by behaviour and lifecycle stage, not by whoever is on the list this week.

Step 02

Build

Copy, subject line, and template assembled — with the test variants defined before anything is scheduled.

Step 03

QA

Links, merge tags, rendering, and suppression rules checked against a fixed pre-send checklist.

Step 04

Report

Opens, clicks, and deliverability reviewed after each send, and fed straight into the next one.
